# MOVE Apayao teaser

## Format

- Portrait: 1080 x 1920
- Target length: 38–42 seconds
- Audience: Pudtol pilot passengers, drivers, and LGU partners
- Voice: warm, reassuring, confident

## Narration

A better way to move is coming to Apayao.

MOVE Apayao begins with the Pudtol–Luna Pilot Loop—a connected electric transport service for e-buses, e-trikes, and e-jeeps.

One platform. Three electric vehicle types. One connected pilot loop.

Passengers can book rides, view estimated fares, and track their assigned vehicle live. Drivers can receive requests, verify pickups, and manage every trip through one connected system.

We’re starting with one loop to test, learn, and improve. Then, guided by real results and community needs, MOVE Apayao can expand to more communities across Apayao.

Green rides. Stronger Apayao. Launching soon.

## On-screen sequence

1. MOVE Apayao title and “A better way to move is coming” launch message
2. Animated Pudtol–Luna pilot route via Payanan, Provincial Capitol, Apayao State College, and Tumog
3. “One platform” vehicle reveal for e-bus, e-trike, and e-jeep
4. Passenger pickup, destination, fare, and live tracking
5. Driver availability, incoming request, pickup verification, and trip completion
6. “Pilot first: test, learn, improve” expansion message
7. “Launching Soon” call to action for the Pudtol–Luna pilot
