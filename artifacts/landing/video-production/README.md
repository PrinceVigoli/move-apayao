recordings stay outside `public/` so they are never shipped with the landing
website by accident.

## Folders

- `raw/` — original portrait phone recordings, without editing
- `exports/` — review copies and editing masters
- `../public/videos/` — final optimized website files only

## Requested recording sequence

1. Sign in and open the passenger home screen.
2. Tap **Book a ride**.
3. Select pickup and destination on the Pudtol–Payanan loop.
4. Select the passenger count and show the estimated fare.
5. Confirm the booking and show driver matching.
6. Show live tracking, driver contact controls, and the pickup PIN.
7. Complete the trip and submit a rating.

Record vertically at 1080 × 1920 with notifications hidden. Use demo data only.
