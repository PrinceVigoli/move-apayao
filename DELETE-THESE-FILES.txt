--- Cash fare model (top-up removed) ---

PowerShell, from the repo root:

  Remove-Item replit.md, CHANGES.md, LOCAL_SETUP.md -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
  Remove-Item artifacts\api-server\src\lib\payment-provider.ts -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
  Remove-Item "artifacts\mobile\app\(tabs)\wallet.tsx" -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
  Remove-Item -Recurse artifacts\mobile\app\transaction -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
  Remove-Item artifacts\dashboard\src\pages\wallets.tsx -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue

Then:

  pnpm install --no-frozen-lockfile
  pnpm run typecheck

Your .env.local files are NOT in this zip, so your existing ones stay untouched.
